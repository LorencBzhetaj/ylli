# Project rules
- Read PROMPT.md, docs/SPEC.md, docs/INPUTS.md, docs/ACCEPTANCE.md and config/dedication.json before coding.
- Preserve the approved mockup in assets/approved-mockup.png: ivory certificate, ivory map sheet with navy circular map, premium astronomical portrait, navy/gold cover, ivory letter and card. No lion illustrations. Do not reproduce generated illegible text.
- All recipient-facing text Albanian, with correct diacritics. Private symbolic dedication, never official astronomical naming.
- Work in milestones, report tests and blockers; do not deploy, push, purchase, or upload private assets without explicit approval. No fake completed work.
- Mobile-first, accessible, reduced-motion fallback, no forced audio. No hardcoded secrets. Avoid unnecessary paid APIs. Keep assets and dependencies licensed.
